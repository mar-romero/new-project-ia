# Human Decision Escalation

Do not silently decide on behalf of the human when a choice affects production
credentials, destructive operations, security posture, recurring cost,
irreversible architecture, compliance or other material risk.

Present a decision packet with context, why it matters, viable options with
benefits/risks/cost/reversibility, a recommendation only when evidence supports
one, evidence, and what happens next. Then stop for direction.
